Deadlines

  • Author registration: 10 August 2026 (AEST)
  • Non-author registration: 22 September 2026 (AEST)

Requirement

  • At least one author of each accepted paper must complete a Full Registration by the author registration deadline. One full registration covers one accepted paper.
  • Where an author has multiple accepted papers, additional registrations are required.
  • Papers without a valid author registration may be withdrawn from the proceedings and program.

Details subject to final confirmation.

Cancellation and Refund Policy

  • Cancellation requests must be submitted in writing.
  • Refunds are subject to approval by the Organising Committee.
  • A 20% processing fee applies.
  • Refunds will be made to the original payment method.
  • Author registrations associated with accepted papers are generally non-refundable.

Transfers and Substitutions

  • Registration transfer is allowed up to 21 days before the conference, with written notice to the registration team.
  • Transfers are allowed between participants only.
  • Registrations cannot be transferred to future conference editions.
