General Co-Chairs

Etienne Borde University of Canterbury, New Zealand

Etienne Borde holds a PhD from TELECOM Paris, Institut Polytechnique de Paris, France, and a Habilitation from Sorbonne Université, Paris, France. His main topics of interest are the configuration, optimization, and reconfiguration of software intensive critical systems, based on their architectural models. His current work focuses on cybersecurity, including moving target defence, cyber threat intelligence, and software vulnerability detection.

Mengmeng Ge Monash University, Australia

Mengmeng Ge received her PhD degree from the University of Canterbury, New Zealand, in 2018. Prior to joining Monash, she was a Senior Lecturer at the University of Canterbury. She also has four years of industry experience, including roles as an Analytics Engineer at Telstra and a Senior Cybersecurity Consultant at Deloitte. Her research expertise lies in evaluation-based security modelling, intrusion detection, moving target defence, cyber threat intelligence, and cybersecurity education.
